## MARKET TRENDS — 2025

- Dubai property prices rose approximately 8–12% year-on-year
- Off-plan (pre-construction) properties are selling fast with 20–40% down payment options
- Payment plans: many developers offer 1% per month post-handover
- Golden Visa: property purchase of AED 2M+ qualifies for UAE Golden Visa (10-year residency)
- Prime areas (Downtown, Palm, MBR City) have very limited availability
- Dubai Marina and JBR: high rental yields of 5–7% annually
- JVC: highest rental yields in Dubai at 7–9% for investors
- Abu Dhabi market growing steadily, 6–8% YoY appreciation
- Most popular off-plan developers: Emaar, DAMAC, Aldar, Meraas, Sobha

---

## COMMON FEES

- Dubai Land Department (DLD) Transfer Fee: 4% of property value
- Agency/Brokerage Fee: 2% (buyer) + 2% (seller) — sometimes 2% total negotiable
- Service Charges (maintenance): AED 10–25 per sq ft per year depending on building
- NOC (No Objection Certificate) fee: AED 500–5,000 depending on developer
- Mortgage registration fee: 0.25% of loan amount
- Mortgage arrangement fee: 1% of loan amount (bank)
- Title deed issuance: AED 4,000

## PROPERTY TYPES EXPLAINED

- **Freehold**: Foreigners can own 100% — available in designated zones (Dubai Marina, Downtown, Palm, JVC, etc.)
- **Leasehold**: 99-year lease, no full ownership — mainly older Abu Dhabi areas
- **Off-plan**: Buying directly from developer before completion — usually 10–30% below market
- **Ready**: Completed and available for immediate occupancy or rental
- **Studio**: Typically 350–600 sq ft, suitable for singles/couples
- **1BR**: 700–1,000 sq ft typically
- **2BR**: 1,000–1,600 sq ft typically
- **3BR**: 1,500–2,500 sq ft typically
- **Townhouse**: 2–3 floors, 1,500–3,000 sq ft, small garden
- **Villa**: Standalone, 2,500–10,000+ sq ft, private garden and parking

## UK MARKET CONTEXT (2025)

- Average UK house price: approx. £290,000 (Halifax/Nationwide index)
- London average: approx. £540,000
- Stamp Duty Land Tax (SDLT): 0% up to £125K; 2% to £250K; 5% to £925K; 10% to £1.5M; 12% above
- Additional 3% surcharge on second homes and buy-to-let
- Agent fees (letting): 8–15% of annual rent for full management
- Average rental yield UK: 4–6%; Manchester/Liverpool: 6–8%
- Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) required for all sales and lettings
- Leasehold vs Freehold: houses typically freehold; flats typically leasehold (often 99–999 years)

## US MARKET CONTEXT (2025)

- Median US home price: approx. $420,000 (NAR data)
- 30-year fixed mortgage rate: approximately 6.5–7.0% (2025)
- Property tax varies by state: Texas ~1.6–2.5%, California ~1.1%, Florida ~0.8–1.2%, New York ~1.5–2.5%
- Closing costs: typically 2–5% of purchase price (buyer); 6–8% (seller including agent fees)
- Agent commission: traditionally 5–6% split between buyer's and seller's agent
- Popular investor markets: Miami, Austin, Phoenix, Nashville — strong rental demand
- Foreign buyers: no restrictions on purchasing US property; FIRPTA withholding 15% on sale
- HOA fees (condos/communities): $200 – $1,500+/month depending on amenities
- Investment yields: Houston/Dallas 5–7%, Miami 4–6%, NYC/LA 3–4%
